Most AI proposal tools have amnesia.

They write a decent first draft — then forget the client ever existed. You end up re-explaining the same person, every single time.

Every other tool

  • Blank page, every time — even for a client you've pitched five times
  • Generic output that reads like it was written for anyone
  • You retype budget, tone, and context into the prompt — again
  • No idea why the last proposal won or lost

Pitchora

  • Remembers each client's budget, tone, industry, and decision-makers
  • Learns what won and what lost — with confidence scores
  • Injects all of it into every new draft, automatically
  • Proposal #5 is measurably sharper than proposal #1

The more you use it, the more it knows.

That's the whole point. Two layers of memory per client — stable facts and learned patterns — quietly working behind every draft.

  • Anchor facts

    Budget range, preferred tone, industry, currency, who actually signs off. Set once, kept current.

  • Scored learnings

    After every signed or declined proposal, the AI extracts atomic learnings — preferences, constraints, win patterns — each with a confidence score.

  • Auto-injected, always fresh

    Memory flows into every new draft's prompt without you lifting a finger. Unreinforced learnings decay, so stale assumptions fade out on their own.

  • Yours, and only yours

    Memory is private to your workspace. Edit anything, export everything (GDPR-ready), or forget a client irreversibly in one click.
